Material density explained

Density is mass per unit volume.

Core principle

The equation ρ = m/V expresses density in kilograms per cubic meter when using SI units.

Understanding the concept

An object with volume 0.002 m³ and mass 5 kg has density 2500 kg/m³.

Practical context

Density is useful for comparing materials, but does not independently describe strength or stiffness.
