Newtons laws

Newton's laws describe classical motion.

Core principle

Objects maintain constant velocity when net force is zero. For constant mass, net force equals mass times acceleration. Interaction forces occur in equal and opposite pairs on different bodies.

Practical context

A book resting on a table has balanced gravity and support forces.
