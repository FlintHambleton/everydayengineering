Electrical power explained

Electrical power is the rate of energy transfer in an electrical system.

Core principle

For steady DC, P = VI. A theoretical device drawing 0.2 A at 5 V consumes 1 W.

Understanding the concept

Energy is power multiplied by time. Operating at 1 W for three hours consumes 3 Wh.

Practical context

Power and energy are different: watts describe rate and watt-hours describe accumulated energy.
