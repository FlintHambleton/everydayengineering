How electric motors work

Electric motors convert electrical energy to mechanical rotation.

Core principle

Magnetic fields interact with current-carrying conductors or magnets to produce forces. Motor designs arrange these forces to create rotation.

Understanding the concept

Some motors use electronic switching to maintain the appropriate magnetic field alignment; others use induction or mechanical commutation.

Practical context

Energy losses include winding heating, friction and magnetic losses.
