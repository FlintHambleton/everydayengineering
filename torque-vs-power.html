Torque vs. power

Torque measures rotational turning effect; power measures the rate of energy transfer.

Core principle

Torque equals perpendicular force times distance from the axis. A 10 N force at a radius of 0.2 m creates 2 N·m of torque.

Understanding the concept

Rotational power P = τω, where angular speed is in radians per second. At 5 rad/s, 2 N·m corresponds to 10 W.

Practical context

Two motors with the same torque can deliver different power if they rotate at different speeds.
