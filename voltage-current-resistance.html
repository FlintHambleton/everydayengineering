Voltage, current and resistance

These quantities describe different electrical properties.

Core principle

Voltage is energy transferred per unit charge, current is charge flow per second, and resistance describes opposition to current.

Understanding the concept

For an ohmic resistor V = IR. A theoretical 5 V across 100 Ω gives 0.05 A.

Practical context

Not every electrical component obeys a constant-resistance relationship.
