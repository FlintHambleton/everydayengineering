Hardness vs. toughness

Hardness and toughness describe different material responses.

Core principle

Hardness measures resistance to localized indentation or scratching, depending on the test.

Understanding the concept

Toughness relates to energy absorbed before fracture. A hard material is not necessarily tough.

Practical context

Glass illustrates how a material can resist scratching yet fracture with relatively little deformation.
