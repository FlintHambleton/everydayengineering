Series vs. parallel circuits

Circuit connections determine how current and voltage are distributed.

Core principle

Series components share a current; parallel branches share voltage across their endpoints.

Understanding the concept

Two ideal 100 Ω resistors in series total 200 Ω, while the same resistors in parallel total 50 Ω.

Practical context

These are mathematical ideal-circuit examples, not instructions for working on energized equipment.
