AC vs. DC electricity

Direct current has a consistent direction; alternating current changes direction periodically.

Core principle

A battery provides a DC voltage, while alternating voltage can be represented by a sinusoid.

Understanding the concept

Frequency counts cycles per second. A 60 Hz sinusoid completes 60 cycles each second.

Practical context

Electronic devices often use DC internally even when their power source is AC.
