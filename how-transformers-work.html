How transformers work

Transformers exchange energy between circuits through changing magnetic flux.

Core principle

Alternating current in one winding establishes changing magnetic flux that induces voltage in another winding.

Understanding the concept

For an ideal transformer the voltage ratio equals the turns ratio, and input and output power are equal.

Practical context

Real transformers have heat and magnetic losses; ordinary steady DC cannot sustain transformer action.
