How springs work

Springs store elastic energy as they deform.

Core principle

For an ideal linear spring, force magnitude F = kx, where k is stiffness in N/m and x is displacement in meters.

Understanding the concept

Stored energy is kx²/2. A 100 N/m spring compressed 0.02 m stores 0.02 J.

Practical context

A spring becomes nonlinear or permanently deformed outside its intended elastic range.
