Kinetic energy explained

Kinetic energy is energy associated with motion.

Core principle

For nonrelativistic translational motion, KE = mv²/2.

Understanding the concept

A 2 kg object moving at 3 m/s has 9 J of kinetic energy. Doubling speed quadruples kinetic energy at constant mass.

Practical context

Rotating objects also possess rotational kinetic energy.
