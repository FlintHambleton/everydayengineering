How 3d printing works

Additive manufacturing builds objects by adding material.

Core principle

Processes include deposited filament, cured resin and fused powder. Digital models are divided into layers.

Practical context

Layer orientation influences finish and strength. Printed prototypes are not automatically suitable for structural use.
