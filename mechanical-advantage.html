Mechanical advantage explained

Mechanical advantage compares output force with input force.

Core principle

For an ideal lever, mechanical advantage equals input arm length divided by output arm length.

Understanding the concept

A 30 cm input arm and 10 cm output arm give a ratio of three. The output travels less distance than the input.

Practical context

A seesaw balances when the moments about its pivot cancel.
