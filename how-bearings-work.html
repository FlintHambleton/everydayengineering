How bearings work

Bearings support motion while limiting unwanted movement.

Core principle

Rolling bearings use balls or rollers between surfaces. Plain bearings instead allow sliding on a suitable surface.

Understanding the concept

Radial loads act across a shaft; axial loads act along it. Bearing geometry determines which loads it can accommodate.

Practical context

A desk fan and a drawer slide illustrate different ways of guiding motion.
