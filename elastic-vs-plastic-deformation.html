Elastic vs. plastic deformation

Materials can change shape reversibly or permanently.

Core principle

Elastic deformation disappears when the load is removed. Plastic deformation leaves a residual change in shape.

Understanding the concept

The elastic modulus relates stress to strain in the linear elastic region. It describes stiffness rather than ultimate strength.

Practical context

A gently bent paper strip and creased paper demonstrate the distinction qualitatively.
