Why metals corrode

Corrosion is degradation through chemical or electrochemical interaction with the environment.

Core principle

Iron can react with oxygen and moisture to form rust. Some metals develop protective surface oxides instead.

Understanding the concept

Salt and moisture can accelerate electrochemical processes; different metals in electrical contact may experience galvanic corrosion.

Practical context

Coatings and material selection are common engineering responses to corrosion.
