How friction works

Friction opposes relative sliding or the tendency to slide.

Core principle

A simple dry-friction model gives maximum static friction as μsN. Sliding friction is often approximated as μkN.

Understanding the concept

With normal force 10 N and coefficient 0.3, the model predicts 3 N of friction at the relevant limit or sliding condition.

Practical context

Real friction depends on surface condition, temperature, lubrication and other factors.
