What is cad

Computer-aided design represents objects and assemblies digitally.

Core principle

Parametric modeling defines dimensions and relationships; direct modeling changes geometry more directly.

Practical context

CAD supports drawings and planning, but a model alone does not establish safety or manufacturability.
