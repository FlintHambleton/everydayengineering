Tensile strength explained

Tensile strength describes resistance to failure under tension.

Core principle

Engineering stress equals force divided by original cross-sectional area. Strain is extension divided by original length.

Understanding the concept

Ultimate tensile strength is the highest engineering stress reached during a tensile test. Yield strength describes the onset of specified permanent deformation.

Practical context

These values are not interchangeable with allowable design stress.
