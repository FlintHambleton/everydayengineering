How pulleys work

Pulleys redirect tension and can distribute a load across several rope segments.

Core principle

A fixed ideal pulley changes direction without changing force magnitude. A movable ideal pulley supported by two rope segments halves the required force.

Understanding the concept

The trade-off is distance: pulling two meters of rope moves the ideal load one meter. Friction increases real input force.

Practical context

A classroom model with a small weight demonstrates conservation of work.
