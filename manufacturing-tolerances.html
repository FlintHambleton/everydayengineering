Manufacturing tolerances

Tolerances specify permitted dimensional variation.

Core principle

A nominal 20 mm dimension with ±0.1 mm tolerance permits 19.9–20.1 mm.

Practical context

Tight tolerances can increase cost; dimensions should reflect functional requirements.
