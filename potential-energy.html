Potential energy

Potential energy arises from configuration in a conservative force field.

Core principle

Near Earth's surface, gravitational energy change = mgΔh. Raising a 1 kg object by 0.5 m increases its energy by about 4.9 J.

Practical context

The reference height is arbitrary; energy differences matter.
