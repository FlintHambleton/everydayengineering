Work energy power

Work transfers energy; power measures the rate of transfer.

Core principle

A constant force aligned with motion does work equal to force times distance. A 4 N force over 2 m does 8 J of work; over 4 seconds this averages 2 W.

Practical context

Joules measure energy and watts measure power.
