Aluminum vs. steel

Aluminum and steel are families of materials with distinct properties.

Core principle

Typical aluminum alloys have density near 2700 kg/m³; common steels near 7850 kg/m³. Equal volumes therefore differ substantially in mass.

Understanding the concept

Steel typically has a higher elastic modulus, but strength depends on alloy and treatment. Geometry also influences stiffness.

Practical context

Material selection depends on corrosion, mass, manufacturing cost and the actual load case.
